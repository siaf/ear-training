Frequently asked

Why lifetime?

Because some of us are done renting software we use for a decade. If you're like that, I built this tier for you. First 100 seats only — after that, subscriptions are still here, but lifetime is done.

What if I haven't played in 10 years?

Start at level 1. Nothing here assumes current chops, but nothing patronizes you either. The curriculum is built for adults — no cartoon owls, no "you're on fire!" popups.

Do I need to practice every day?

No. Streaks are off by default on the returner tier. Practice three days one week, one day the next — it all gets measured the same way.

What if Fifths shuts down?

Fair question. If the company folds, I'll open-source the app and ship a downloadable offline build. Your practice data is yours — exportable to CSV any time.

Why only 100 lifetime seats?

Because I want the validation signal. If 100 adults pay $99, I know this product matters. If they don't, I'd rather find out now than after building for a year.

When does it launch?

Live now at app.fifths.io. The $99 lifetime is reserved for the first 100 seats — once they're claimed, the tier closes forever.
