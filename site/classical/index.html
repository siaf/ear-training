Frequently asked

Does this support movable-do?

Yes, by default. Fixed-do is available as a setting. Your solfege system, your call.

Does it work for choral sight-reading?

Single-voice at launch. Multi-voice (SATB passage scoring) is on the post-launch roadmap — 3–6 months out.

How does the functional-analysis work?

Chords are labeled with Roman numerals in context (I, ii°, V7/V, bII — even Neapolitans and augmented sixths at higher levels). The matrix tracks which functions you mis-identify most often.

Is there a free tier?

Yes. Full baseline curriculum + 7-day analytics. Pro adds advanced modules, unlimited history, mic input, and auto-drill.
