Instrument samples used by the free tools on fifths.io
=======================================================

Neither set needs a credit; this file records where they came from.
The files are trimmed, mono, 96 kbps copies made by scripts/build-samples.sh
(leading silence removed, shortened with a fade-out, re-encoded as MP3).

piano/  Steinway B grand piano, close mics, middle velocity layer, from the
        Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
        Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).

guitar/ Steel-string acoustic guitar, University of Iowa Electronic Music
        Studios Musical Instrument Samples, https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
        ("freely available ... may be downloaded and used for any projects,
        without restrictions"). Prepared by nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments.
