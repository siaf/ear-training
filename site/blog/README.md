# The Fifths science blog

Ear training as a practice has a long pedagogical history and, in the last few decades, a growing scientific literature alongside it. This series is our attempt to translate that literature — perceptual learning, cognitive psychology, music-education research — into practical guidance for self-directed learners and into transparent reasoning behind how Fifths is built.

Every article cites primary sources. Where the evidence is contested or weaker than the popular framing, we say so.
