Frequently asked

I've done EarMaster. Why didn't that work?

Because almost all interval drills play notes in a vacuum. Real songs have a key. Without that reference, intervals sound different every time you hear them. We put the key back in. That's the whole trick.

How long does it take to hear scale degrees?

Most people get reliable 1-3-5 (the tonic triad) in a week of 15-min/day practice. All 7 scale degrees in 4–6 weeks. The matrix shows you when you're getting close.

Do I need perfect pitch?

No. This is pure relative — scale degrees in a given key. Perfect pitch isn't required and isn't taught.
