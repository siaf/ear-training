# Fifths Google Ads — Keyword Plan

Source: 6 Keyword Planner CSVs in [ads-research/](../ads-research/) (`fifths`, `seeds`, `earmaster`, `completeeartrainer`, `sonofield`, `tonegym`), targeting US+CA+UK+AU, English, last 12mo.

**Filters applied:** ear-training-relevant stems only, exclude irrelevant terms (free / youtube / school / kids / books / app store / amazon / etc.), ≥50 monthly searches, Low/Medium competition, top-of-page bid (low) ≤ $3.

**Total relevant pool:** ~440 keywords across 12 themes. **Launch picks:** 6 ad groups, 38 keywords (each in exact + phrase variants → 76 total).

---

## Strategic notes from the data

1. **`relative pitch` is wide-open.** 5,000/mo, Low competition, **$0 top-of-page bid floor**. No one is bidding on it currently. Highest-confidence recommendation in the whole plan.
2. **`scale degrees` is unclaimed too.** 5,000/mo at $0 floor. Niche but Fifths' [/classical/](../site/classical/index.html) and broader curriculum cover this directly.
3. **`ear training` head term is contested but not crowded.** 5,000/mo Low, $0.63 floor. EarMaster, Sonofield, ToneGym, CompleteEarTrainer all show up — but bid floor is low, suggesting CPC is reachable.
4. **`perfect pitch` is a trap.** 50,000/mo and tempting, but Fifths teaches RELATIVE pitch. Searchers wanting "perfect pitch" mostly fail (it's largely innate) and the ones who succeed are wired differently from our user base. Skip the head term, keep only `relative pitch`/`pitch training` variants.
5. **`solfege` is high volume but ambiguous.** 50,000/mo at $0.48 floor — but solfege searchers split between (a) academic students looking for free study material and (b) musicians who want practical interval recognition. Test cautiously with one keyword + the [/classical/](../site/classical/index.html) landing.
6. **`transcribe music` mostly leaks to "audio→sheet music auto-conversion"** apps (Klangio, AnthemScore). Skip generic transcription terms; keep only "by ear" / training-oriented ones.
7. **`functional ear trainer`** is a competitor (a free Mac app) that gets 500/mo of organic search. Direct competitor-name targeting in a separate group.

Why each:
- `free / pdf / youtube / tutorial / how to`: content-seekers, not app-shoppers
- `kids / children / school / classroom`: Fifths is for adult musicians past beginner level
- `beginner / course / lesson`: implies they want a curriculum-style course, not a drill app
- `sheet music / chord chart / guitar tabs`: different intent (printable resources)
- `vs / review of`: comparison shoppers (different funnel stage; harder to convert from a search ad)
- `apk / crack / mod / torrent / download`: pirates
- `song / songs / lyrics`: leaks into music-discovery search (Shazam/Genius territory)
- `chord progression / guitar chord / piano chord`: instrument-specific learning, not ear training

## Bidding & budget

**Daily budget:** $10 to start. Tight enough to limit damage if Quality Score is bad initially; loose enough to actually buy traffic on the Low-bid keywords.

**Bidding:**
- Start with **Maximize Clicks** with bid cap $1.20 — Smart Bidding has zero conversion data to learn from, so let it acquire clicks while we collect signal.
- Manual CPC override per ad group:
  - `competitor-brand`: cap $1.50 (these CPCs run higher)
  - `solfege-classical`: cap $1.00 (volume is high, intent is mixed; cap protects budget)
  - all other groups: default $1.20 cap is fine

**Switch points:**
- After **10 conversions** (any of the 3 actions): switch to **Maximize Conversions**.
- After **30 paid conversions** of `fifths_purchase` specifically: switch to **tCPA** with target = $30 (proxy for first-year LTV).

**Geography:** US + Canada + UK + Australia, "Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations" (NOT "interest in").

## Expected first-week metrics

Rough projections at $10/day across 6 ad groups, 38 keywords:

| Metric | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Daily impressions | 50–200 (most volume sits on `ear training` + `solfege` + `transcribe music`) |
| Daily clicks | 8–20 |
| CTR | 5–10% (good RSAs on tight ad groups) |
| Avg CPC | $0.40–$0.80 |
| `fifths_engaged` events from clicks | 1–4/day (assuming ~30% complete a session) |
| `fifths_signup` events from clicks | 0.3–1/day (assuming ~5% sign up) |
| `fifths_purchase` events from clicks | ~0/week initially — paid conversion compounds slowly |

**Quality Score check at week 1:** open Keywords tab, sort by QS. Anything ≤4 is unhealthy. Most likely culprit: ad group too broad (mixed themes confuse the algorithm). Tighten by splitting groups or adding negatives.

**Search-term report at week 1:** open Keywords → Search terms. Look for queries that triggered our ads but feel off-topic. Add as negatives. Repeat weekly.

## What to do if budget burns with no conversions

If after **$50–80 spent** you've got 0 `fifths_purchase` events AND 0 `fifths_signup` events:

1. **Don't increase budget** — that compounds the loss.
2. **Check tracking is firing** — incognito click on an ad → land on app.fifths.io → use the app → check Tag Assistant for the conversion event.
3. **Check landing page bounce rate** in GA4. If >80%, the ad copy promised something the landing doesn't deliver (rare given Fifths' alignment, but possible).
4. **Pause `solfege-classical`** — most likely the noisy group.
5. **Check Quality Score** — if a keyword is QS ≤3 with high impression count, that's the burn source.

If after $150 with 0 conversions: pause Campaign 1 entirely, audit the funnel, decide whether the issue is targeting (try competitor-only campaign at $5/day) or product fit (rare given organic data).
